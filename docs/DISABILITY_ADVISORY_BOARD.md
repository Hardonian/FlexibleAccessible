# Disability Advisory Board Charter - AROS

**Effective Date:** 2026-04-10  
**Status:** Active

---

## Purpose

The Disability Advisory Board ensures AccessibleMadeFlexible represents and serves the disability community with authentic perspectives and rigorous accessibility standards.

---

## Board Composition

### Required Representation

| Seat | Perspective | Term |
|------|-------------|------|
| 1 | Visual impairments | 2 years |
| 1 | Motor/mobility | 2 years |
| 1 | Cognitive/neurological | 2 years |
| 1 | Deaf/hard of hearing | 2 years |
| 1 | Chronic illness/pain | 2 years |
| 1 | Advocate/industry expert | 2 years |

### Diversity Requirements

- Minimum 3 members from underrepresented backgrounds
- Geographic diversity (minimum 2 regions)
- Technology experience diversity (expert + novice)

---

## Responsibilities

### Advisory

1. **Product Feedback:** Review features for accessibility impact
2. **Research Input:** Guide user research methodologies
3. **Testing:** Participate in beta testing with assistive technology
4. **Policy:** Advise on accessibility claims and boundaries
5. **Hiring:** Input on accessibility-focused roles

### Not Responsibilities

- ❌ Board does not make product decisions
- ❌ Board does not approve marketing
- ❌ Board has no veto power
- ❌ Board is not responsible for compliance

## Compensation

| Role | Compensation |
|------|---------------|
| Member | $500/stipend per quarter |
| Chair | $750/stipend per quarter |
| Travel | Covered for in-person |

### Stipend Terms

- Quarterly payment for active participation
- 2 meetings minimum required for payment
- Emeritus status for inactive members

---

## Selection Process

### Nomination

1. Open call (30 days notice)
2. Community nomination form
3. Self-nomination allowed
4. Panel review

### Criteria

- Direct lived experience (preferred) OR
- Extensive advocacy work
- Technology accessibility knowledge
- Available for quarterly participation

### Selection

| Phase | Timeline |
|-------|----------|
| Nominations open | 30 days before term |
| Review | 2 weeks |
| Selection | 1 week |
| Announcement | 1 week |

---

## Governance

### Reporting

Board reports to:
- Product Lead (quarterly)
- CEO (annually)

### Documentation

- Meeting notes published (annonymized)
- Annual impact report

### Removal

| Reason | Process |
|--------|---------|
| Non-participation | 2 consecutive meetings → review |
| Conduct violation | Immediate review |
| Misrepresentation | Panel decision |
