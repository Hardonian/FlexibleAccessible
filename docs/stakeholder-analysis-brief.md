## Critical Success Factors

1. **Executive sponsorship** - C-level champion with authority to remove barriers
2. **Dedicated PM ownership** - 0.25 FTE minimum sustained engagement
3. **Underrepresented voice amplification** - Proactive outreach, not passive waiting
4. **Accessibility of engagement** - WCAG AA for all communications; AT testing
5. **Closed-loop feedback** - Every input gets acknowledgment and response
