# AROS: Enterprise Objection Battlecards & Positioning

Tactical sales arguments and objection handling designed to close commercial deals against competitors.

---

## 1. Competitor Comparison Battlecards

### Battlecard 1: vs JavaScript Overlays (accessiBe, UserWay, AudioEye)

- **Competitor Claim**: *"Just install one line of JavaScript and you're 100% compliant with zero developer work."*
- **The Reality**:
  - Overlays do not fix underlying source code defects.
  - Screen reader users actively dislike and frequently block them.
  - In 2023–2025, over 800 federal ADA Title III lawsuits specifically named websites running accessibility overlays.
- **AROS Killing Point**:
  > *"Overlays put a temporary sticker on a broken engine. When someone sues or an enterprise auditor inspects your source code, the overlay is legally indefensible. AROS generates real, git-committed source code fixes that repair your application at the root, making your product genuinely accessible and legally sound."*

---

### Battlecard 2: vs Legacy Consulting Firms (Level Access, Deque, Crownpeak)

- **Competitor Claim**: *"Pay us $35,000–$75,000 for a 6-week manual audit and static PDF report."*
- **The Reality**:
  - Prohibitively expensive and slow.
  - The static report becomes obsolete the moment your developers merge their next sprint pull request.
  - Leaves the hardest part—actually writing code fixes—entirely to the customer.
- **AROS Killing Point**:
  > *"Consultants tell you what is broken and charge you five figures to hand you a spreadsheet. AROS not only scans your app continuously in your CI/CD pipeline, but provides the exact code remedies, GitHub PR integration, and live VPAT exports at 1/10th the cost."*

## 2. Common Enterprise Buyer Objections

### Objection 1: "Can your AI accidentally break our production site?"

**Response**:
> *"Never. AROS does not inject dynamic code into your production runtime. All AI-assisted remedies undergo deterministic schema and AST validation, are staged as standard Git Pull Requests in your repository, and require explicit developer review and sign-off before anything is merged."*

---

### Objection 2: "We are a small startup; can't we just ignore accessibility until we're bigger?"

**Response**:
> *"In California, New York, and Florida, automated ADA lawsuit bots target companies as soon as they raise funding or gain traffic. Settling an ADA demand letter typically costs $15,000–$40,000 in legal fees alone, plus mandatory remediation under a tight deadline. Running AROS from day one costs $49–$149/mo and prevents the lawsuit from ever landing on your desk."*

---

### Objection 3: "Does AROS guarantee 100% legal immunity?"

**Response**:
> *"No honest engineering company can claim '100% automated immunity' because true accessibility involves both automated and human experiential evaluation. What AROS provides is an evidence-backed audit trail: continuous scan records, remediation lifecycle tracking, and an exportable VPAT 2.5 matrix that proves rigorous, good-faith compliance and proactive due diligence."*
