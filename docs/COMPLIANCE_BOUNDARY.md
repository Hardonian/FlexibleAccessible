# Compliance Boundary Language - AROS

**Effective Date:** 2026-04-10  
**Status:** Required - All Customer-Facing Content

---

## Core Principle

**AccessibleMadeFlexible NEVER claims WCAG compliance or guaranteed accessibility outcomes.**

Automated scanning cannot prove full WCAG conformance. Claiming otherwise is a legal liability.

---

## Required Disclaimers

### On Homepage & Marketing

> AccessibleMadeFlexible provides tools for *discovering* and *remediating* accessibility issues. We do **not guarantee** WCAG compliance or certification. Full compliance requires manual expert review.

### On Scan Results

> These results are generated by automated scanning (axe-core). Automated tools detect approximately 30-40% of WCAG criteria. Manual expert review is required for compliance verification.

### On AI Remediation Suggestions

> AI-generated suggestions are automated recommendations. Every fix requires human review before implementation. We do not guarantee that suggested fixes resolve accessibility issues.

### On Reports & Exports

> This report reflects automated scan results only. It does not constitute a WCAG compliance audit or certification. For compliance verification, engage a qualified accessibility expert for manual review.

| Understandable | ~25% |
| Robust | ~50% |
| **Overall** | **~30-40%** |

### Non-Detectable Issues

The following require manual review:

- **Cognitive accessibility** - Complex language, inconsistent navigation
- **Multimedia** - Video descriptions, captions timing
- **Temporal** - Time limits, auto-playing content
- **Content clarity** - Meaning, purpose, relationships
- **Keyboard traps** - Complex interaction patterns
- **Focus visibility** - Subtle focus indicators

### Required Language on Coverage

> AccessibleMadeFlexible uses axe-core for automated scanning. axe-core detects approximately 30-40% of WCAG criteria. A comprehensive accessibility audit requires manual expert review of criteria that cannot be automated.

## AI Remediation Guardrails

### Confidence Scoring

All AI suggestions include:

| Score | Meaning | Required Action |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| High (0.8-1.0) | Likely effective | Human review |
| Medium (0.5-0.79) | Possible | Human review required |
| Low (<0.5) | Uncertain | Extra scrutiny required |

### Required Display

Every AI suggestion must show:

```
Suggested Fix: [code]
Confidence: 0.72 (Medium)
⚠️ Human review required before implementation
```

## Contract Language (Enterprise)

### Required Terms

> **HUMAN REVIEW REQUIREMENT:** Customer agrees that all automated findings and AI-generated remediation suggestions require review by qualified accessibility professionals before implementation.

> **NO CERTIFICATION:** Supplier does not provide WCAG compliance certification. Any certification requires independent audit by accredited accessibility expert.
