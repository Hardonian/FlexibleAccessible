## Messaging House
### Core narrative
AccessibleMadeFlexible turns accessibility from periodic audits into an operating workflow: discover, verify, prioritize, remediate, and re-check with tenant-safe controls.

### Pillars
1. **Operational continuity:** recurring workflow, not one-time scan theater.
2. **Trust boundaries:** explicit server-side entitlements and tenant-scoped access.
3. **Evidence orientation:** trackable findings/remediation lifecycle.

## Competitive Framing
- Versus “score-only” scanners: emphasize operator workflows and recurring remediation.
- Versus overlays: emphasize root-cause remediation orientation.
- Versus heavyweight enterprise suites: emphasize implementation speed and clear operator control.

## Why-Us / Why-Now / Why-Not-Us
- **Why us:** Practical, implementation-grounded remediation OS with real billing/paywall primitives.
- **Why now:** Accessibility debt compounds with release velocity; recurring operations matter.
- **Why not us:** If buyer requires fully audited enterprise attestations today, current trust package may be insufficient.
