# Baseline Forecast Template & Scenario Planning

**Status:** RECOMMENDED + ASSUMPTION  
**Purpose:** Create disciplined planning frames without fabricated performance claims.  
**Scope:** Unit economics assumptions, lean/realistic/aggressive scenarios.

## Unit Economics Inputs (assumption fields)
- Paid org count by tier
- ARPA by tier (from official price book)
- Monthly churn by tier
- Gross margin components: hosting, queue/DB, AI API, support labor
- Acquisition spend by channel
- Onboarding/service hours per customer segment

## Scenario Definitions
- **Lean:** slower conversion, higher churn, conservative expansion.
- **Realistic:** moderate conversion + moderate retention improvements.
- **Aggressive:** stronger activation and expansion with controlled support load.

## Forecast Output Template (monthly)
1. Opening paid orgs
2. New paid orgs
3. Expansion upgrades
4. Churned orgs
5. Ending paid orgs
6. MRR run-rate (assumption-based)
7. Direct cost load
8. Contribution margin
9. Cash runway impact

## Controls
- No forecast may publish as "commit" without documenting input assumptions and owner.
- Reconcile forecast vs actual in monthly founder review.

## External Review
- **REQUIRES EXTERNAL REVIEW:** Tax treatment, rev-rec policy interpretations, and formal financial statement presentation.
