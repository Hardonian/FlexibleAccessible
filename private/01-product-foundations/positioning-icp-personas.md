# Positioning, ICP, and Persona Map

**Status:** RECOMMENDED  
**Purpose:** Anchor commercialization around the real product and buying motion.  
**Scope:** ICP, buyer/user/operator roles, why-now framing.

## Current Truth
AccessibleMadeFlexible is strongest where a team needs continuous accessibility remediation operations, not one-off scans.

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)
1. **Primary ICP (now):** Product teams, digital agencies, and accessibility-minded engineering leads managing 1–10 domains with ongoing release cadence.
2. **Secondary ICP (now):** Mid-market organizations that need evidence trails and recurring verification but can tolerate manual onboarding support.
3. **Not ICP yet:** Highly regulated buyers requiring formal attestations not yet evidenced by implementation artifacts.

## Persona Map
| Persona | Primary pain | Product value | Commercial note |
|---|---|---|---|
| Economic buyer (Founder/Director) | Risk + recurring remediation cost | Ongoing visibility and workflow structure | Needs proof of operational ROI, not compliance guarantees |
| Technical buyer (Eng Manager/Lead Dev) | Accessibility debt in CI/release flow | Crawl → scan → findings → remediation flow | Wants credible integration and low false confidence |
| Operator (Accessibility lead/QA) | Manual triage overhead | Clustering, evidence traces, review workflows | Needs repeatability + audit trail |
| Agency operator | Serving multiple client sites | Repeatable scanning/remediation delivery process | High fit for service + software hybrid motion |

## Why Us / Why Now / Why Not Us
- **Why us:** Product-enforced tenant and entitlement boundaries, evidence-centric remediation workflow.
- **Why now:** Teams already shipping frequently need recurring accessibility operations.
- **Why not us (truthful):** If buyer needs guaranteed compliance certification with formal attestation package today, implementation and control evidence are not complete enough yet.
