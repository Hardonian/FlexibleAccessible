# Business Operating Cadence + Weekly/Monthly Checklists

**Status:** RECOMMENDED  
**Purpose:** Define disciplined operating rhythm for solo operator/small team.  
**Scope:** Weekly founder review, monthly business review, dashboard package.

## Weekly Founder Review Checklist
- Commercial:
  - paid conversions, churn, expansion opportunities
  - top objections and win/loss notes
- Product truth:
  - entitlement incidents
  - queue reliability and onboarding blockers
- Customer success:
  - at-risk accounts, past_due recovery
- Finance:
  - cash collection issues, refunds/credits, unresolved recon items
- Execution:
  - top 3 next-week priorities tied to revenue/risk

## Monthly Operating Review Checklist
1. Close books with reconciliation checklist.
2. Review KPI pack (activation, conversion, churn, support attainment).
3. Review assumptions and risk register updates.
4. Confirm roadmap split: maintenance vs leverage vs moat.
5. Decide price/package experiments for next month.

## Dashboard Pack (minimum)
- Revenue and paid org movement summary.
- Activation funnel and first-value milestones.
- Reliability and support incident trend.
- Billing anomalies and failed-payment backlog.
