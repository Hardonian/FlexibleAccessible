# Ownership Matrix, Decision Log, Assumptions Register, Risk Register

**Status:** RECOMMENDED + ASSUMPTION  
**Purpose:** Add explicit ownership and decision hygiene for commercialization.  
**Scope:** RACI-lite matrix, decision template, assumptions and risks.

## Documentation Ownership Matrix
| Area | Owner | Backup | Review cadence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pricing and packaging | Founder/Product | Ops lead | Monthly |
| Billing operations | Founder/Ops | Engineering | Weekly |
| Finance controls | Founder + external accountant | Ops | Monthly |
| Onboarding/support SOP | Customer success owner | Founder | Bi-weekly |
| Trust/procurement docs | Founder + security lead | Engineering | Quarterly |

## Decision Log Template
- Decision ID
- Date
- Decision statement
- Context/problem
- Options considered
- Chosen option and rationale
- Risks accepted
- Follow-up tasks and owner
