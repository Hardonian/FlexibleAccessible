# Agency / White-Glove Delivery Runbook

**Status:** RECOMMENDED  
**Purpose:** Define repeatable services-led motion that complements product subscriptions.  
**Scope:** Agency onboarding package, handoff, boundaries.

## Current Truth
- Architecture supports org-scoped operations suitable for per-client isolation.
- Contractual services can be layered without changing core entitlement model.

## White-Glove Option (recommended offer)
- Kickoff and environment validation
- First-site setup and crawl/scan calibration
- Initial findings triage workshop
- Remediation backlog prioritization and handoff

## Implementation Handoff Checklist
- Confirm owner roles and billing admins.
- Confirm domains, crawl depth/page caps aligned to plan.
- Confirm remediation workflow ownership and cadence.
- Deliver written runbook to client operator.

## Boundaries
- Service scope/time is contractual, not a product entitlement.
- No guarantee of certification/compliance outcomes.
