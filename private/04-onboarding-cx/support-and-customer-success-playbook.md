# Support, Conversion, and Customer Success Playbook

**Status:** RECOMMENDED  
**Purpose:** Provide repeatable support and churn-control execution.  
**Scope:** Severity matrix, conversion playbook, churn prevention/recovery, FAQ objections.

## Support Severity & Response Matrix
| Severity | Example | Target response | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sev 1 | Paid org cannot access private routes despite active billing | < 2h | Founder/operator |
| Sev 2 | Scan queue unavailable for paid org | < 8h | Operator + infra |
| Sev 3 | Feature confusion/UX support | < 1 business day | Support/operator |
| Sev 4 | How-to guidance | < 2 business days | Support/CX |

## Trial-to-Paid Conversion Playbook
1. Detect activation milestone completion but unpaid status.
2. Trigger contextual upgrade prompt (site/scan/remediation action boundary).
3. Offer 30-minute onboarding assist for qualified accounts.
4. Log objection reason code and conversion outcome.

## Churn Prevention / Recovery
- Weekly at-risk review: past_due, low usage, repeated failures.
- Outreach templates by risk type:
  - payment issue,
  - onboarding stalled,
  - feature expectation mismatch.
- Recovery offers: onboarding session, temporary grace period (with explicit stop date), plan-rightsizing.

## Objection Handling Matrix (internal)
| Objection | Truthful response |
|---|---|
| “Can you guarantee compliance?” | No. We provide operational remediation and evidence workflow, not guaranteed certification. |
| “Why paid if free scan exists?” | Free scan is discovery; private ongoing remediation operations are paid and enforced server-side. |
| “Do you support enterprise controls?” | Some controls exist; attestations/advanced enterprise commitments depend on explicit roadmap and proof pack. |

## Customer Education Outline
- Day 0: setup + first value
- Week 1: findings triage + remediation workflow
- Week 2+: recurring scan cadence, governance/reporting habits
